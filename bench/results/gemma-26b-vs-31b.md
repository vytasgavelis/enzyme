same prompt (`createCardGenerator`), scored with Mastra scorers (`apps/server/src/bench/scorers.ts`).
Wall time of the last session 57 s with at most 2 model calls at once.

Summary faithfulness is an LLM judge; no model judges its own cards:
- `google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it` cards were judged by `google/gemma-4-31b-it`.
- `google/gemma-4-31b-it` cards were judged by `google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`.

How the scores work:
- **Quote check rate**: of the fields the model filled in, the share whose quote `checkCard` found verbatim in the title or abstract.
- **Unknown discipline**: of the five fields abstracts rarely state (comparator, dose, duration, limitations, funding), the share the model either left unknown or backed with a verified quote. A dose or funding value also counts as a guess when the quote isn't a dose or funding statement, or a number in the value isn't in the abstract.
- **Summary faithfulness**: the judge splits the takeaway and plain summary into claims and marks each as supported by the verified facts or not; the score pools all claims.

## Conclusions

We chose Gemma 4 26B-A4B over Gemma 4 31B as Enzyme's card model. We ran both on the same 12 papers about inflammation, autoimmunity and gut health. On extraction they were level: 99% of both models' quotes were found word for word in the abstract, and both nearly always said "unknown" instead of guessing. 26B-A4B made one slip, giving the route ("oral supplementation") as a dose. But 26B-A4B wrote a card in a median 14 s against 49 s, and it never timed out. 31B timed out twice at our 90 s limit, so 26B-A4B produced 11 of 12 cards to 31B's 10. To grade the takeaway and plain summary, we had each model judge the other's cards, so no model marked its own work. 26B-A4B's claims were supported 84% of the time and 31B's 69%. The gap is soft: the judges differ, and most flagged claims were true to the abstract but went beyond the extracted facts (for example "no adverse events were observed" or "no limitations were stated"). Still, it gives no reason to pay 3.5 times the wait for the bigger model.
